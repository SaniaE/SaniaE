## Hi I'm Sania 👋


I'm a Machine Learning Engineer with an engineering mindset and an artistic streak. At work, I design and optimize AI systems, balancing experimentation, performance, and real-world constraints. My work spans Adaptive Ensembles and LLM-powered applications, as well as developing and improving ML workflows through MLOps, monitoring, evaluation, and explainability.

Beyond work, I learn by building, experimenting, and connecting ideas, often venturing into concepts simply because I find them interesting. My interests span different areas of AI and machine learning, including Generative AI, Explainable AI, Reinforcement Learning, Computer Vision, and Deep Learning. 

What fascinates me about machine learning is its probabilistic nature. Building reliable systems when outcomes aren't always deterministic is a challenge that drew me to the field and continues to shape how I approach problems.

I believe the best ideas rarely arrive fully formed. They're usually hiding somewhere between a failed experiment, an unexpected observation, and a question you almost didn't bother asking. This GitHub is where some of those interests take shape: a collection of things I've built, things I'm exploring, and ideas I'm putting to the test. A space for turning interesting questions and ideas into something tangible.
